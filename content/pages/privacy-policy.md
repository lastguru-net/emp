This Privacy Policy explains the limited processing of personal data that occurs when you visit this website.

Last updated: 27 August 2026

## Information processed when you visit

This is a static website. It has no user accounts, contact forms, analytics, or advertising trackers, and the operator does not maintain separate visitor logs or attempt to identify visitors.

The following service providers receive technical information when your browser requests the website and its fonts:

- **Cloudflare** delivers and protects the website. It processes information such as your IP address, the requested URL, request time, HTTP headers, and security-related data for content delivery, security, and abuse prevention. Cloudflare processes this information in accordance with its [Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
- **Google Fonts** provides the font files used by the website. When your browser requests a font file from Google, Google receives your IP address and request information. Google states that the Google Fonts API does not set or log cookies. Further information is available in the [Google Fonts privacy FAQ](https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy).

Cloudflare and Google may process information outside the European Economic Area. Information about their processing and international-transfer safeguards is available in their respective privacy documentation.

## Cookies and browser storage

This website does **not** set or use cookies.

It stores the current light or dark theme in your browser's local storage under the key `theme`. This value remains on your device and is not transmitted to the website operator. You can remove it by clearing the website's stored data in your browser. If you remove it, the website will select a theme again on your next visit.

## Purposes and legal basis

The technical information described above is processed only to deliver the website and its fonts, maintain security, and prevent abuse. The legal basis is the operator's legitimate interest in providing a functional and secure website, under Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR.

The operator does not use the information for analytics, advertising, profiling, or identifying individual visitors.

## Retention

The operator does not create or retain separate visitor logs. Retention by Cloudflare and Google is governed by their respective policies and applicable service settings.

## Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, the GDPR may give you rights to access, rectify, erase, or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to object to processing based on legitimate interests.

The operator does not maintain information that directly identifies visitors and does not attempt to associate technical requests with particular people. Under Article 11 of the GDPR, the operator is not required to collect additional information solely to identify a person who wishes to exercise their rights. As a result, the operator may be unable to locate information relating to you unless you provide sufficient additional information to identify it.

You may also lodge a complaint with the data-protection supervisory authority in the country where you live or work, or where you believe an infringement occurred.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website's data-processing practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
